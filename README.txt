Hotel Booking Smart Contract

A decentralized hotel room booking system built in Solidity for the Ethereum blockchain.

=> About the Project
Instead of relying on traditional booking websites that charge high commission fees and control your data, this project uses an Ethereum Smart Contract. The contract automatically manages room inventory, processes payments in Ether safely, and confirms room reservations without any middleman.

- Module: Distributed Digital Transactions (BSc 3rd Year)
- Institution: CCT College Dublin
- Technology Stack: Solidity, Ethereum, Remix IDE, Git/GitHub


---

=> How It Works in 3 Simple Steps
1. The Hotel Owner: registers rooms with a name and diária price in Ether.
2. The Guest: selects a room, chooses the number of nights, and sends the exact payment in Ether.
3. The Smart Contract: checks the payment, locks in the booking, and holds the funds safely until the owner withdraws them.
